# Detour: tester guide

A message you can send to friends. Replace the link if the address changes.

---

Hi! I'm building **Detour**, a tiny app that turns a minute of scrolling into things you actually remember. Could you try it for a couple of weeks and tell me what you think?

**Install it (30 seconds, no account, no app store)**

1. Open https://danpina.github.io/detour/ on your phone.
2. **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu, then **Install app**.
3. Open it from your Home Screen. Pick 3 or more topics and play your first set (about a minute).

**What would help most**

- Use it a little most days. The app brings cards back for a quick recall check after a day or more, so come back tomorrow.
- Tell me when something is confusing, wrong or boring. Every card has a **Report** button for mistakes, and Settings has a feedback link.
- Tell me honestly: would you want this to pop up before you open a social app?

**Good to know**

- Your progress stays on your phone. There are no accounts, and the app only sends anonymous counts (see Settings → Privacy). You can turn that off.
- Settings → **Export progress** saves a backup file. Safari can clear a website's data after about a week of not using it, and installing to the Home Screen avoids that.
- Settings → **Daily reminder** adds a repeating calendar alert, if you'd like a nudge.

Thank you!
