# Spanish, advanced: verbs and adjectives
aprovechar | to make the most of | verbs | Hay que aprovechar cada oportunidad. | You have to make the most of every opportunity.
lograr | to achieve | verbs | Queremos lograr un cambio real. | We want to achieve real change.
alcanzar | to reach | verbs | Esperan alcanzar el objetivo este año. | They hope to reach the goal this year.
afrontar | to face | verbs | Tenemos que afrontar la realidad. | We have to face reality.
superar | to overcome | verbs | Logró superar el miedo a volar. | She managed to overcome her fear of flying.
plantear | to raise, to put forward | verbs | Voy a plantear el problema en la reunión. | I am going to raise the problem at the meeting.
abordar | to tackle | verbs | Es hora de abordar el tema con seriedad. | It is time to tackle the subject seriously.
respaldar | to back, to support | verbs | Varios países decidieron respaldar la propuesta. | Several countries decided to back the proposal.
impulsar | to drive, to boost | verbs | El gobierno quiere impulsar la industria. | The government wants to boost industry.
fomentar | to encourage | verbs | Hay que fomentar la lectura entre los jóvenes. | Reading among young people must be encouraged.
cumplir | to fulfil | verbs | Es importante cumplir lo que se promete. | It is important to keep what you promise.
incumplir | to break (a promise, a rule) | verbs | Es un error incumplir el contrato. | It is a mistake to break the contract.
rechazar | to reject | verbs | La comisión decidió rechazar la oferta. | The committee decided to reject the offer.
advertir | to warn | verbs | Los expertos quieren advertir del riesgo. | The experts want to warn about the risk.
reconocer | to recognise, to admit | verbs | Hay que reconocer los propios errores. | You have to admit your own mistakes.
suponer | to suppose, to mean | verbs | No quiero suponer nada sin pruebas. | I don't want to assume anything without proof.
asumir | to take on | verbs | Debes asumir tu responsabilidad. | You must take on your responsibility.
mantener | to maintain | verbs | Es difícil mantener la calma. | It is hard to stay calm.
evitar | to avoid | verbs | Intento evitar los conflictos. | I try to avoid conflicts.
provocar | to cause | verbs | La sequía puede provocar una crisis. | The drought can cause a crisis.
desarrollar | to develop | verbs | Quieren desarrollar un nuevo producto. | They want to develop a new product.
destacar | to stand out | verbs | Vamos a destacar los puntos clave. | We are going to highlight the key points.
acordar | to agree | verbs | Las partes lograron acordar un precio. | The parties managed to agree on a price.
negociar | to negotiate | verbs | Decidieron negociar con el sindicato. | They decided to negotiate with the union.
reclamar | to claim, to demand | verbs | Tiene derecho a reclamar una indemnización. | He has the right to claim compensation.
denunciar | to report, to denounce | verbs | Decidió denunciar el robo. | She decided to report the theft.
sancionar | to penalise | verbs | La federación va a sancionar al club. | The federation is going to penalise the club.
investigar | to investigate | verbs | La policía va a investigar el caso. | The police are going to investigate the case.
demostrar | to prove | verbs | Los datos permiten demostrar la teoría. | The data make it possible to prove the theory.
comprobar | to check | verbs | Voy a comprobar los datos otra vez. | I am going to check the data again.
confirmar | to confirm | verbs | Necesito confirmar la reserva. | I need to confirm the booking.
aclarar | to clarify | verbs | Permíteme aclarar un detalle. | Allow me to clarify one detail.
resumir | to summarise | verbs | Intento resumir el texto en pocas líneas. | I try to summarise the text in a few lines.
valorar | to value, to assess | verbs | Hay que valorar los pros y los contras. | You have to weigh the pros and cons.
comparar | to compare | verbs | Voy a comparar los precios. | I am going to compare prices.
criticar | to criticise | verbs | Es fácil criticar y difícil proponer. | It is easy to criticise and hard to propose.
defender | to defend | verbs | Voy a defender mi punto de vista. | I am going to defend my point of view.
convencer | to convince | verbs | Quiero convencer a mis socios. | I want to convince my partners.
persuadir | to persuade | verbs | Intentó persuadir al jurado. | He tried to persuade the jury.
sorprender | to surprise | verbs | Su respuesta me va a sorprender. | His answer is going to surprise me.
decepcionar | to disappoint | verbs | No quiero decepcionar a mi equipo. | I don't want to disappoint my team.
agradecer | to thank | verbs | Quiero agradecer su paciencia. | I want to thank you for your patience.
disculparse | to apologise | verbs | Es importante disculparse a tiempo. | It is important to apologise in time.
arrepentirse | to regret | verbs | Es inútil arrepentirse ahora. | It is useless to regret it now.
rendirse | to give up | verbs | Es pronto para rendirse. | It is too early to give up.
esforzarse | to make an effort | verbs | Hay que esforzarse para aprender. | You have to make an effort to learn.
adaptarse | to adapt | verbs | Es necesario adaptarse al cambio. | It is necessary to adapt to change.
acostumbrarse | to get used to | verbs | Es difícil acostumbrarse a la ciudad. | It is hard to get used to the city.
enterarse | to find out | verbs | Es mejor enterarse temprano. | It is better to find out early.
darse cuenta | to realise | verbs | Tarde o temprano va a darse cuenta. | Sooner or later he is going to realise.
tratarse de | to be about | verbs | Todo parece tratarse de dinero. | It all seems to be about money.
quejarse | to complain | verbs | Es inútil quejarse del tiempo. | It is pointless to complain about the weather.
atreverse | to dare | verbs | Es difícil atreverse a hablar. | It is hard to dare to speak.
oponerse | to oppose | verbs | Los vecinos van a oponerse al proyecto. | The neighbours are going to oppose the project.
imprescindible | essential | adjectives | Es imprescindible llegar a tiempo. | It is essential to arrive on time.
inevitable | unavoidable | adjectives | El cambio es inevitable. | Change is unavoidable.
fiable | reliable | adjectives | Es una fuente fiable. | It is a reliable source.
riguroso | rigorous | adjectives | El estudio es muy riguroso. | The study is very rigorous.
eficaz | effective | adjectives | Es un método eficaz. | It is an effective method.
rentable | profitable | adjectives | El negocio es muy rentable. | The business is very profitable.
imprevisto | unforeseen | adjectives | Fue un contratiempo imprevisto. | It was an unforeseen setback.
alarmante | alarming | adjectives | La cifra es alarmante. | The figure is alarming.
escaso | scarce | adjectives | El agua es un recurso escaso. | Water is a scarce resource.
abundante | abundant | adjectives | La lluvia fue abundante este año. | The rain was abundant this year.
sutil | subtle | adjectives | Hay una diferencia sutil entre ambas. | There is a subtle difference between the two.
evidente | obvious | adjectives | Es evidente que hay un error. | It is obvious that there is a mistake.
dudoso | doubtful | adjectives | El resultado es dudoso. | The result is doubtful.
razonable | reasonable | adjectives | Su propuesta es razonable. | His proposal is reasonable.
exigente | demanding | adjectives | Mi jefa es muy exigente. | My boss is very demanding.
paciente | patient | adjectives | Es un profesor muy paciente. | He is a very patient teacher.
testarudo | stubborn | adjectives | Mi hermano es testarudo. | My brother is stubborn.
sincero | sincere | adjectives | Quiero ser sincero contigo. | I want to be honest with you.
honesto | honest | adjectives | Es un hombre honesto. | He is an honest man.
generoso | generous | adjectives | Es muy generoso con su tiempo. | He is very generous with his time.
ingenioso | ingenious | adjectives | Es un plan ingenioso. | It is an ingenious plan.
cotidiano | everyday | adjectives | Es un problema cotidiano. | It is an everyday problem.
habitual | usual | adjectives | Es mi ruta habitual. | It is my usual route.
vigente | in force | adjectives | La norma sigue vigente. | The rule is still in force.
precario | precarious | adjectives | Tiene un empleo precario. | He has a precarious job.
pleno | full, complete | adjectives | Estamos en pleno invierno. | We are in the middle of winter.
ajeno | someone else's | adjectives | El dolor ajeno nos importa. | Other people's pain matters to us.
propio | own | adjectives | Cada uno tiene su propio ritmo. | Everyone has their own pace.
diario | daily | adjectives | Es un hábito diario. | It is a daily habit.
